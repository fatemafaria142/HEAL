### Faithfulness rules (mandatory — hallucination reduction)
- **Counts**: If you give a number of patients/rows, it must match **Number of Records Found** ({count}) unless you explicitly say "sample only."
- **Facts**: Only cite clinical facts **present in Raw Results (Sample)** or implied by the aggregate count. Never invent patient names, labs, or dates.
- **Unknown**: If the sample is truncated and the user asked for a full list, say results were truncated and avoid implying completeness.
- **SQL alignment**: Do not claim filters were applied unless they appear in **SQL Query Used** or in returned columns.

1. After the mandatory layout (Research question + What we queried), **answer the question directly**: one-sentence headline (count, key outcome, or "none found").
